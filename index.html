About EPAC

Eagle Persistence and Completion — Retention and Graduation Action Team · 2026–2027

🎯
Action Team Charge

The EPAC Action Team will develop and implement annual strategic projects that improve student retention and graduation, and monitor institutional progress toward assigned Performance-Based Funding (PBF) metrics. The team will further identify systemic barriers affecting student persistence, coordinate cross-divisional student success initiatives, recommend policy and technology improvements, evaluate initiative effectiveness, and provide recommendations to the Executive Committee regarding institutional priorities and resource needs.

One team, one goal
"Get every Eagle to the finish line."

Understanding PBF

Performance-Based Funding metrics, FGCU historical performance, and cohort pipeline reference

The Four Metrics EPAC Influences Most

FGCU participates in the Florida State University System Performance-Based Funding (PBF) model. EPAC action work directly supports the persistence and timely-completion outcomes it measures. Definitions follow the Florida Board of Governors 2025 metric definitions.

Metric 4
Four-Year FTIC Graduation Rate

First-time-in-college students enrolled full-time in their first semester who graduated from FGCU within four academic years.

Metric 5
Academic Progress Rate (APR)

FTIC students who return the following Fall and hold at least a 2.0 cumulative GPA after their first academic year.

Metric 9A
Three-Year Grad Rate — FCS AA Transfers

Students entering from the Florida College System with an AA degree who graduate from FGCU within three academic years. Full- and part-time students included.

Metric 9B
Six-Year Grad Rate — First-Year Pell Students

Students who received a Pell Grant during their first year and graduated from FGCU within six academic years. Full- and part-time students included.
